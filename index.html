BEHIND THE NAME

OUR STORY

MORE THAN A NAME.

PHEZ’KWE comes from the expression “Phezulu kwe Touchline” — on top of the touchline.

The touchline represents the place where the game continues to be watched, learned and understood. It reminds us that we remain present, whether we win or lose.

Our journey is shaped by culture, creativity, resilience and the lessons that come with every experience.
